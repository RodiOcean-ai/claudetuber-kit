# The faceless-channel house style, measured (9 videos, 29 Oct 2026)

## Format and pace
- 1920×1080, **60 fps**. Long videos 11-22 min (median 17.5).
- **14 cuts a minute** (10.7-22), average shot 4.3 s, median shot 2.1 s. 10-28 % of shots are under 1 s.
- Only 9-14 % of shots run over 10 s, but they fill 36-60 % of the time: screen recordings, mind-maps and line-art kept alive by eased zooms, pans, slow 3D tilt and pop-in labels. **Rule: something new appears every 2-4 s, even inside one shot.**

| Section | Pace |
|---|---|
| Tutorial / screen recording | 10-90 s without a cut; a zoom, label or pop every 2-4 s |
| Example or montage burst | 0.5-1.5 s per shot (20-29 cuts in 30 s) |
| Skit / story drawings | 3-6 s per drawing; endings use long dissolves |
| Chapter map | one continuous animated move, 10-60 s |
| Cold open | first hard cut at 2-15 s (about 3 s typical) |

## Text
- Captions: white bold sans, 1-4 words, low in the frame (about 88 % height), dark shadow; small and sparse in his newer videos.
- Punch words: centred, ALL CAPS heavy geometric sans, white or the main colour with a soft glow; one word per beat, often on black.
- Story subtitles: small italic serif, cream, bottom centre.
- Newer kinetic type: white sans mixed with a coloured italic serif word; stacked and scaled; **hand-drawn red circles, arrows and underlines** on screenshots.
- **Signature opener:** a viewer comment card (dark rounded box, avatar, handle) fading in on black. Ours: real comments from our own audience only, or a written question card.

## Mascot use (ours, not his)
- Older style: a full-body cut-out entering from the right edge, 40-100 % of the frame height, pointing at the content, every 10-30 s.
- Newer style: a small round head badge in a corner of screen recordings; a line-art version for story skits.
- Outfits may change; face and hair never do (one character sheet).

## Overlay and transition kit
Orange/yellow light-leak flash (screen blend, about every 3-5 min), radial zoom-blur push, 3D-tilted screen recordings, neon outline frames, glowing drawn lines (maps, roadmaps, scribble wipes), sparks, glass shatter, grain, RGB glitch, pixel mosaic, hard cut to one word on black.

## Colour (his, for reference; ours comes from our palette)
Black base; gold/amber primary; cyan secondary; red accent for warnings and arrows; white text. Story drawings shift amber → red-orange at the low point → yellow at the resolution (colour follows the hero's journey).

## Music plan (his main comedy tool)
- A bed under about **90 %** of the running time (83-94 %), mostly upbeat; one calm bed for a calm video.
- **Drop-outs:** 5-15 clear ones per long video on a strict count (5-30 counting small ones). The bed cuts to near-silence 1-3 s before or on a punchline, meta joke or skit line, then slams back 10-20 dB louder on the next sentence.
- Sponsor skit: 2-3 drops on its punchlines; the bed returns after the thank-you line.
- **The ending turn:** 7-8 s of near-silence on a "But..." line, then a new soft bed (piano, horns) under a reflective story section, often with no hard cuts for 1.5-3 min.
- Openers: either quiet (voice at 2-3 s) or sound effects only with no voice for 6-10 s; his older videos started talking at 0 s.
- Never copy his silences caused by copyright mutes (I08 has 67 s of digital silence).

## Sound effects
2.2-9.5 a minute counted (median 4.3; the real number is higher). Kit: whoosh/swipe on transitions; click or camera shutter on pop-ups; typing under screen recordings; boom/riser on reveals; heartbeat for tension; coins for money; gasp; comic stingers (horn blat, bass pluck, animal noise).

## Loudness
- His mix: -18 LUFS (median), voice 8.8 LU above music+effects (5.6-12.2: about 6 for hype, 11-12 for teaching), loudness range about 4.7 LU, peaks up to +0.9 dB (clipping).
- **Ours:** keep his voice-to-music gap (9 LU general, 6 hype, 11-12 teaching) but master to -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP.
- Voice speed overall 2.4-3.7 words/s (median 3.2); the voice covers 68-89 % of the time.
