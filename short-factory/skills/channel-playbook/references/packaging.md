Rules: first person; 5-8 words; Title Case; "Actually" signals "no guru fluff, the real process"; a number + time frame for challenges; "!" on challenge titles only; no emoji.
His four best are the four "How I Actually..." tutorials: practical promise + personal proof.

```
🎸 [music]: [link]
...

⏳ Timestamps:
00:00 Intro
01:15 [chapter written as a search phrase: "How to X"]
...

#youtube #[topic]
```
- Chapters are written as search phrases ("How to find outlier videos on youtube").
- A free lead magnet (guide/preset pack) in almost every video.
- Sponsor line first when there is a sponsor.
- Tags: 12-22 search phrases (the topic in several wordings, "how to X", his channel name spelled 3 ways). We add our channel name the same way and never his.
- Our addition: an "AI voice / AI images used" line where AI is used.
