# The 10 thumbnail types (his best 10, from a list of 21 by another creator)

| # | Type | What it is | Target feeling | His note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shock** | one jaw-dropping moment from the video (a chicken being fired into a jet engine) | "wait, what?" | works for any niche, not only entertainment; find what in your video would shock |
| 2 | **Big things / big numbers** | a huge price, count or size ("$50M house", "20M VIEWS") | greed, awe | write the number big; it should be the real number from the video |
| 3 | **Simple** | one plain object or screenshot + a short question (a chair, a red button, a chat message) | curiosity | stands out among loud thumbnails; he names the format benchmark channel as a master of it |
| 4 | **Social hacking** | the face of a famous person in the niche | recognition | only if the video is really about that person; otherwise it's clickbait |
| 5 | **Creator + something bigger** | the creator/mascot next to a big object or result | "what's he doing?" | the one his channel uses |
| 6 | **Header** | one interesting line of text across the top for context | clarity | his most-used text style |
| 7 | **Comparison** | before/after, cheap vs expensive, A vs B | "which wins?" | two halves, clear divider |
| 8 | **Blur / hidden** | blur or pixelate the key thing or the result | must click to see | his I04 thumbnail: pixelated face on a phone |
| 9 | **Branded** | a logo or brand mark that people know | trust | his example: a chair recognisable from its brand |
| 10 | **Weird object** | something odd that doesn't belong | "what is that?" | often merges with Simple |

**Combine 2-3.** Every viral thumbnail he showed combines types (a "buy every iPhone" thumbnail = big numbers + header + comparison + social hacking). His own: creator + big number + header (I05), creator + hidden + big number (I04), creator + simple prop (I03).

**Don't copy the niche.** If many channels already use one look for this topic, it's the cliché now and their audience already has it. Look at other niches for the idea; keep only our own colours, font and mascot constant.
