# Structures, scripts and the picture plan

## A. What-if compilation (8-12 min, 6-10 segments)

**Order:**
| Slot | What goes there |
|---|---|
| 1 | The strongest "question as a picture" (e.g. a giant hand pouring water on the Sun). It starts at 0 s; the premise is never explained. |
| 2-3 | One light science what-if, then one darker one |
| middle | Alternate light/dark; one "pure dread" segment with no music for contrast |
| ~80 % | **The thumbnail segment** (held back; the chapter list shows it's coming) |
| last | The most mind-bending what-if or a twist ending. No CTA, no outro. |

**Segment template (45-90 s, 120-230 words at 2.55 words/s):**
1. **Premise (0-8 s):** "If you…" / "What if every…" / "Imagine you…", with the stakes inside the question.
2. **Obvious answer (8-15 s):** "Well, normally…", "You might think…", shown with a normal-world picture (a fire put out by water).
3. **Reversal (15-20 s):** "But [X] isn't a normal [Y]."
4. **The mechanism, in 2-4 escalating steps (20-50 s):** "You see,…", "Now,…", "And with every…". One picture per clause, the camera flying inside the thing.
5. **The consequence, bigger than expected (last 10 s),** ending on the segment's biggest line ("…everyone on the planet would be cooked").
6. **Exit:** an object or camera wipe straight into the next segment's premise line. No pause.

## B. One what-if deep dive (5-7 min)
1. **Cold open (0-25 s):** the premise + the stakes on the most extreme picture: "If the Earth stopped spinning for just one second, [the stakes]." The music swells. **Black beat:** 1-2 s of black with near-silence.
2. **Ground rules (25-60 s):** "To understand why, you need to know one thing about…". One simple diagram-style picture in the blue explainer world.
3. **Escalation ladder (60 s to the end minus 60 s):** 4-7 time or scale stages, each with its own picture world and a timeline counter on screen ("1 second later", "1 hour later", "1 year later"). Each stage ends on a pivot line. **Plant a second thread** in stage 1 or 2 (a small detail: a person, a place) that returns in the last stage.
4. **Payoff (last 60 s):** the held-back worst or most surprising picture, and the planted thread pays off.
5. **Kicker:** one dry or ironic closing fact. Stop.

## C. True "harmless thing, terrible outcome" story (5-6 min)
Follow L01 (balloons):
1. A cold open with the climax picture and a "fatal" stakes line.
2. A black beat, then a restart from the beginning.
3. Why they did it.
4. The preparations.
5. **Plant the parallel thread** (the missing fishermen).
6. The launch: "triumph", with a tension cue (a heartbeat) under it.
7. The turn: "this moment of triumph did not last long".
8. The chaos.
9. The threads meet; the death is stated plainly with the music low.
10. An ironic epilogue.

Recreated scenes must be labelled "dramatized" in the description. Real names and real deaths need respectful, accurate facts and sources.

## Pivot-line phrase bank (his words; use the pattern, not always the exact words)
- But there's just one problem.
- And this is where things went terribly wrong.
- But this moment didn't last long.
- Case closed. Well, not exactly.
- Meanwhile, [somewhere else]…
- Well, it turns out…
- You see, …
- Now, here's the strange part.
- And that's when [someone] noticed something.
- Until [year].

## Speech maths
- Words = minutes × 60 × 2.55.
- Voice covers about 90 % of the time.
- Plan black beats and drops on top of that: each one 0.5-2 s, about 3-6 in a 6-minute video.

## Picture plan (cost control)
- A new view every 2-4 s: about 15-25 views per minute. A view is NOT always a new generation. Reuse with a zoom, a pan, a crop or a parallax layer.
- Per segment (A) or per section (B/C):
  - 1 hero video shot (Lite) or 2-3 (Rich), 4-8 s each, on the segment's money moment;
  - 6-10 new stills, each used 1-3 times through different moves;
  - free Remotion graphics for numbers, maps, timelines and labels (sticky notes, glow outlines, arrows; his style).

## Music prompts (Eleven Music v2.5, instrumental)
- **Science / what-if:** "Light, curious documentary underscore, soft marimba and plucked strings over a warm synth pad, steady 105 BPM, even energy, no vocals, no build-up, loops cleanly." (90 s)
- **Story / dread:** "Dark, tense documentary score, low bowed strings, cello and double bass, sparse plucked guitar, slow 70 BPM, a quiet heartbeat-like pulse, no vocals, no big climax, loops cleanly." (120 s)
- **Wonder payoff:** "Slow, awe-filled ambient pads with a soft high piano motif, 80 BPM, gentle swell, no vocals, no drums." (60 s)
- **Mood swaps:** crossfade beds over 1-2 s on a whoosh, never mid-sentence.
