# Packaging: thumbnail, title, description

## Thumbnail (16:9, the click is decided here)
His rules, from 10 thumbnails:
- **One strange, bright image** that asks a question by itself: balloons swallowing a city like an explosion, a bloody bottle cap, a giant grinning host holding a "Newton's cradle" of tiny people, the logo seen from behind.
- **No text in 7 of 10.** When there is text: 2-3 huge words in white with a dark outline ("STUCK GAS PEDAL", "I'M A GENIUS!"). Never repeat the title.
- **Scale contrast:** giant vs tiny, or one object made impossible.
- **Colour:** a saturated blue sky, or red and blood-orange for danger. One focal point. High contrast, readable at 160 px wide.
- **An arrow** (3 of 10): a thick white curved arrow pointing at the detail that makes the question.
- **It shows the held-back segment or moment.** The viewer must sit through the video to see it.
- **Avoid:** busy collages of many faces (his weakest compilation used one).

Our extra lessons, from the Didit thumbnails:
- rule of thirds;
- palette = one main colour + navy + white + one highlight;
- darken behind any text;
- keep the face clear;
- check at a small size.

**Thumbnail prompt (gpt-image-2.5-sunburst, high or medium, 2K, 16:9):**
> YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, one bold focal image: [THE IMPOSSIBLE MOMENT, e.g. a giant smiling host (from the character sheet) dangling tiny people like a Newton's cradle]. Extreme scale contrast, saturated pale-to-deep blue sky gradient background, crisp semi-realistic 3D style, strong rim light, high contrast, subject filling the centre and right third, clean negative space on the left. [Optional: a thick white curved arrow pointing at [detail].] No text, no logos.

Make 2 versions (A/B) and use YouTube's thumbnail test if available.

## Title formulas (from his 10)
- **Paradox story:** `[Place]'s [Year] [Harmless Thing] Disaster` (Ohio's 1986 Balloon Disaster, 20M).
- **Deadly or costly:** `The [Brand/Thing] [Event] That Killed [N] People` / `[Company]'s $[number written in full] Mistake`. Write the full number: "$2,300,000,000" looks bigger than "$2.3B".
- **Compilation:** `[N] [Exclusive Word] [Videos/What-Ifs] I Thought Would [Outcome]`. A number + insider access + a confession.
- **Hidden story:** `The [Disturbing/Dark/Full] Story Behind [Familiar Thing]`.
- **Deep-dive what-if:** `What If [Impossible Thing] For Just [Short Time]?` or `What Would Happen If [Everyone/the Earth] [Did X]?`
- 4-9 words. No emoji in long titles (he uses none). Title Case.

## Description template
```
[One or two sentences that tease the premise and end on a question.]

Sources 👉 [link to the public sources document]

Jump To A Chapter 👇
00:00 [Segment or section 1 title]
00:45 [Segment 2 title]
…

Some scenes in this video are AI-animated and dramatized for illustration.
[Optional: the owner's Patreon/socials line]
```
- Chapters must start at 00:00 and need at least 3 entries of 10 s or more, so YouTube shows them.
- **Tags:** 6-12 literal search phrases a viewer would type (his older story videos had them; the newer ones rely on the title).
- Category: Education, or Film & Animation.

## After posting
- Pin a comment that asks the question again (not a call to subscribe).
- Cut 2-3 vertical shorts from the best segments within the week, each with shorts-format captions, pointing to the full video.
- Record the views at 24 h, 7 days and 28 days, plus the click-through rate and average view duration from YouTube Studio, in `JOURNEY.md`.
