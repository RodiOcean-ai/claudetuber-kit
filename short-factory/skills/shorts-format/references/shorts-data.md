## Channel
- 29.4M subscribers and 4,099 shorts. The median short has **14M views**, so the format, not the title, carries the views.
- Style changed around 2023. The 2022 hits (McFlurry spoon, the magic trick) used real footage, stock clips and his own face at a microphone. Since then everything is his own semi-real 3D.

Shared by all 10:
- the voice starts within 0.5 s (caption already on screen at 0.4 s);
- 0 pauses over 0.5 s in 9 of 10;
- mix loudness -8.4 to -15.1 LUFS (median about -11);
- true peaks above 0 dBTP, so he clips. We don't.

## Hook frames (0-3 s), what they show
- The subject is alone in the centre, already moving: a needle threading at 0.0 s, a finger jabbing wood at 0.0 s, veins sliding out of a body at 0.4 s, a gun firing at 0.4 s, a POV toenail clip at 0.0 s.
- The first caption ("If you", "When a firing squad", "Have you ever") is on screen by 0.4 s.
- No title card, no logo, no text other than the captions.

## Transitions
- Frame-difference analysis found **no hard cuts in 7 of 10**. A scene change is a 2-3 frame whip or swipe with heavy motion blur (wood → blue sky in the splinter short), or the camera flies through or along an object (from a rifle barrel to the prisoner).
- The camera never stops: push-ins, dives into cross-sections, flights over maps and a globe, slow motion on impacts.

## Sound
- **Narrator:** one calm, clear male voice, heavily compressed (an even loudness, range about 2-3 LU), wall-to-wall, about 2.8 words/s.
- **Music bed:**
  - a soft, curious underscore (the classifier hears marimba/xylophone, plucked and light instruments) at about 100-115 BPM;
  - about 16 LU under the voice, present nearly the whole time;
  - no drops in shorts.
- **Effects:**
  - very few: soft whooshes (tagged "zipper") on camera moves, a thud or bang on an impact, a tearing or thread sound on stitching;
  - most shorts have 0-5 in total.

## Captions
- 1-3 words at a time, white bold sans, thin black outline, centred horizontally, about 55-62 % down the frame, small (text height about 3.5-4 % of the frame height).
- Synced word by word to the voice. Unpolished (typos exist), so speed beats perfection.

## Titles, descriptions, covers (top 10 and all 4,099)
- **Title:** 4-8 words (median 6) + one emoji at the end, sometimes "(explained)" or "(How?)".
- **Openings:**
  - "How…" (1,160 shorts), "Why…" (476), "What if / What happens if…" (90), "Can/Could…" (150), "The reason…" (15, the best median at 21M);
  - average views barely differ by opening; a title ending in "?" does a bit better (17M median vs 14M).
- **Emoji, most used:** 🤔 (922), 😮 (792), 😨 (685), 😱 (428), 🤨 (181), 😳 (131), 😬, 🤯, 😢.
- **Descriptions and tags:** none on shorts.
- **Cover:** not designed. A mid-video frame with its caption visible.

- War and weapons: 16M (235 shorts).

- What-if: 15M (157).
- Animals, space and science, planes and cars: 14M each.
