# Creator-breakdown method: a creator's growth told as "luck? no, a plan", in three chained steps

Learned from our study of 4 Nov 2026: the studied strategy channel's top 10 long videos (0.9M-6.8M), 2 weaker same-format videos (161k, 204k), 3 weaker covers, and the titles and views of all 33 of its videos. **The studied channel has no Shorts**, so this skill carries story, packaging and subject choice, never Shorts format. Evidence: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/reference/creators/creator-breakdown/SECRET_SAUCE.md`. Numbers: `STUDY.md`, `techniques.md`, story chart `story_blocks.png` in the same folder.

**Copy the craft, never the identity:** no face, name or voice of his, no whiteboard look, no graph-paper cover design, no catchphrases ("which takes us on to step…", "I'll see you at the top"). No real footage, screenshots or thumbnails of the creators we cover. Faceless toy figures and silhouettes only. The creator stays anonymous until the last line (`reference/lessons.md`).

**Claude builds, the owner clicks.** This skill is free: subject lists, scripts, beat sheets and Blender plans only. Any voice or image generation follows the money rules in `reference/lessons.md`.

**The format benchmark keeps the final word on format.** The studied channel made 10-minute videos. Its pacing (a new picture every 2.5 s, about 3.1 words/s, intros and sign-offs, no loop) does not apply. The shorts-format rules win: 27-35 s, 75-95 words, about 2.8 words/s, voice on frame one, voice about 16 LU above the music, whips, loop to frame one.

## The recipe
1. **Pick the creator like he did** (stage 1). A creator the mass audience knows, **during or just after a fresh growth spike**, with one clean growth number ("4 million subscribers in 3 months", "2.7 million in 30 days"). Mass-audience subjects: median 651k for him; adult-niche (finance, photo, tech, fitness): 115k. Same title, same script: an engineering-stunts creator at his spike 1.2M vs a gaming creator a year after his spike 204k.
2. **Before → after number on frame one.** "800 subscribers a month … then 4 million in 3 months." His hits put the number at 6-20 s (his repeating leak, hook critic 5.9); we put it **on screen at 0 s**.
3. **The belief flip** (8 of 10 hits): "You'd think he just got lucky." → "But it was planned." For us that's the WRONG BELIEF and the "But" twist of the format spine, at 5-9 s.
4. **The planted question** (7 of 10): "What does he know that the rest of us don't?" Spoken or implied, it's the question the whole Short answers.
5. **Three steps chained with "But… so…"** (7 of 10): each step creates the problem the next one solves. "That got him the clicks. But how did he keep them? So he…" This is And-But-Therefore, our default structure.
6. **The last step is the surprising one, and it gets a name** (best-last 5 of 10, counter-intuitive 4 of 10): a kid creator's blurry thumbnails "on purpose", the engineering-stunts creator grew by uploading *less*. Name it in 2-3 words ("statement plus intrigue"). That's the payoff.
7. **Proof cards, not claims:** a glowing view or subscriber card with a green ring or a red arrow on the one number that matters (10 of 10 used a screenshot this way).
8. **The anonymous-hero cover:** the creator never named on the cover (13 of 13), "THE KID WHO BROKE YOUTUBE", a red line rising behind him.
9. **Title:** a growth number + time ("Gained N in [time]" median 1.1M; "Broke YouTube" 2.6M). Avoid money-only ("How much does X make?" 297k). Kept inside our question + one emoji pattern: "How Did a Kid Get 4 Million Subscribers in 3 Months? 🤯".

## How to apply it in sf-pipeline
**Stage 1 (subject), the 100-10-1 funnel from his own playbook** (see "His own playbook" in SECRET_SAUCE.md):
1. **List about 30-100 candidate creators and angles** (free text work): outliers at 3-4× their channel's normal from **sf-niche-research**, our own best episodes ("copy yourself first"), and one combination of two formats nobody has joined. Pinned-comment suggestions go into the list; they don't pick the winner on their own.
2. **Cut with the cover test:** can the idea be one compelling anonymous cover? If not, drop it (it kills about two-thirds). Also drop every kids' channel (made for or starring children, including Roblox story channels): owner's rule in lessons.md.
3. **Cut with the idea ceiling and 80 % overlap:** would *most* of our viewers (people who watched our stakes-ladder and game-drama episodes) want this one? Niche-adult creators fail here.
4. **Score the last 10** from 0-2 on: mass-audience (young/general, not a niche adult hobby), fresh spike (growth that's news now), one clean number (before → after in a short time). Prefer 5-6 out of 6.
5. **Glance test on the top 3:** the idea in one short sentence; clear from the cover alone in one second; "would you unpause for this?"; interesting to both a top YouTuber and someone's non-creator parent. Show the owner the top 3, recommend one.
6. Check the number with **fact-checker** before it goes in a title.

**Stage 3 (script):** after the format spine is drafted (**shorts-format**) and **sf-story-craft** has set the villain (the "luck" belief) and the bookend:
- Line 1 = a you-situation + the before number ("If you got 800 subscribers a month, you'd call it a hobby…").
- "But" twist = the after number + "it wasn't luck."
- Steps 1-2 = one sentence each, joined by "But… so…".
- Step 3 = the counter-intuitive one, named.
- Last line = the reveal of who it is, worded so it loops back to frame one.
- Run **creator-breakdown-critic** in the expert panel (it advises; shorts-format-critic leads).

**Stage 5 (3D, skill sf-blender-world):**
- Frame one: the anonymous hero (a faceless figure at a desk, or the subject's stand-in object) with a **glowing before-number card** already on screen; the card flips to the after-number on the "But".
- Each step = one station along the world's line, with a **proof card** (a floating 3D panel with the number, a green ring or a red arrow drawn on in 0.3 s).
- A **red line rising** across the world, climbing at each step (it doubles as our growth "game board"), last jump on the payoff.
- Camera flights between stations (0.4-0.55 s, owner's rule), CHECK overlap pass, loop to frame one.

**Thumbnail (sf-thumbnail):** the faceless hero centred (our rule beats his off-centre face), a red rising line behind, 0-3 glowing words in his "THE KID WHO…" spirit ("BROKE YOUTUBE?"). His team makes up to 50 covers a video: we write **5-8 cover concepts as text first** (free), run the glance test on each, and price only the best 1-2 for the owner. Title + cover together must pose one question only the Short answers.

**Every stage, the "total stranger" rule (Core, Casual, New):** the Short must work for someone who has never seen our channel or the creator. No "last time", no "you know him", no inside jokes from earlier episodes; the Easter-egg bottle is the only callback, and nobody needs it.

## Self-check before showing the owner
- [ ] The creator came out of a 100-10-1 funnel and passed the cover test, the idea ceiling and the glance test
- [ ] The creator is a mass-audience name with a fresh spike and one clean, checked number
- [ ] A total stranger can follow it (no "last time", no assumed knowledge)
- [ ] The before → after number is on screen on frame one
- [ ] The "luck → plan" flip lands as the "But" at 5-9 s
- [ ] Three steps, each handing a problem to the next; the last is surprising and named
- [ ] At most 4 spoken numbers; proof cards carry the rest
- [ ] Shorts format kept: 27-35 s, 75-95 words, +16 LU voice, flights, loop
- [ ] No face, name, footage or screenshot of any real creator; anonymous until the last line
- [ ] Every number is in facts.md with two sources (not the creator's own "I think we might be the fastest")
