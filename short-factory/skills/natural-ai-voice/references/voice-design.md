# Voice Design templates (the owner designs in the app; pick the best of several previews)

The studied creator's own example of the kind of description he uses: a young man in his 20s, American accent, quirky but charismatic style, a lot of emotion throughout; with the guidance turned down a little. Don't copy his narrator; use these as starting points for our own.

## Explainer narrator (for natural mode)
> Native English, General American. Male, mid 20s to early 30s. Studio quality, close microphone, dry room, no reverb. Persona: a quick-witted friend explaining something he's genuinely excited about; playful, a little sarcastic, warm. Speaks with a lot of emotion that changes often: excited, then deadpan, then curious. Timbre: clear, slightly bright, relaxed. Pacing: fast and conversational, with sudden stops for effect.

## Calm storyteller (for format mode and documentaries)
> Native English, General American. Male, late 20s to early 30s. Studio quality, close microphone, dry room, no reverb. Persona: a friendly science explainer telling a friend something fascinating; calm, curious, matter-of-fact, lightly amused. Timbre: clear, bright mid-range. Pacing: brisk and even, about 170 words per minute.

## Mentor / second voice (the inner-voice friend in story videos)
> Native English, General American. Male, early 30s, slightly deeper and slower than the narrator. Studio quality, close microphone. Persona: a laid-back, wise-cracking friend who's seen it all; teasing but kind. Timbre: warm, a touch of gravel. Pacing: relaxed, with confident pauses.
(Make it clearly different from the narrator so viewers can tell them apart with eyes closed.)

## Female options
Swap "Male" for "Female" and adjust the age; keep the persona, timbre and pacing lines.

Save the chosen voice ID in `channel_voice.txt` (and `mentor_voice.txt`).
