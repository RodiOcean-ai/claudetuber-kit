# Voice samples for the free local voice (bring your own)

`scripts/voice_make.py` needs two short recordings of ONE voice, set in `config.json` → `voice_inhouse`:
- a **lively** sample (8-10 s, energetic delivery): the free voice model copies its energy and emphasis;
- a **calm** sample (about 10 s) of the same voice: the read is voice-swapped to it so it sounds like that speaker.

Use your own voice, or a voice you have the right to use. Never clone a real person without their permission.
The author's channel voice samples are not included.
