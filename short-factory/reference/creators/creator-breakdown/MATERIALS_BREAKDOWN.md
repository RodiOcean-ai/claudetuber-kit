# Paddy Galloway: what his top 3 videos are made of (materials breakdown)

Made 7 November 2026 from what we kept after the study: the frame grids (one frame every 4.8-5.3 s, 354 frames in all), the shot sheets, the opening frames, the cut times and the word-timed transcripts. No video was re-downloaded.

The three videos:
- **Beluga**: "How Beluga Gained 4 Million Subscribers in 3 Months (Genius Strategy)", 6.8M views, 10:37, 2021. 120 frames, one every 5.3 s.
- **MrBeast GENIUS**: "Here's why Mr Beast is a GENIUS - How He Grew his YouTube Channel", 6.2M views, 10:18, end of 2019. 120 frames, one every 5.2 s.
- **Squid Game**: "How MrBeast's Squid Game Broke YouTube (Genius Strategy)", 3.9M views, 9:11, 2021. 114 frames, one every 4.8 s.

## The short version
- **About 30 % of every video is screenshots of YouTube itself**: video tiles, titles, thumbnails, view counts, channel pages. They sit small on a white page, and labels are written on around them.
- **About 21 % is his whiteboard**: grey line-drawn crowds, flat colour icons and hand-drawn charts, with 2-5-word labels in capitals.
- **About 17 % is the creator's own footage**, usually played in one block of 15-40 s with its own sound, as "the example".
- **Real data graphs are only 4 %**, about 2-3 per video. They come at two moments: the hook (the growth jump) and the turn (the problem).
- **Paddy never appears on camera** (0 of 354 frames). The narrator is a voice only. When a face is needed, it is the creator's face cut out of a photo and put on white.
- **The look is one white page.** About 80 % of all frames have a white background. The camera pans and zooms across one long white page instead of cutting. In Beluga, from 6:51 to 10:00 is a single "shot" of 189 s. Almost all of the real cuts happen inside the creator's clips.

### Rhythm
- **The material changes every 11-14 s**, but the picture changes about every 2.5 s, because labels, arrows and ellipses keep being added to the same page.
- **A new screenshot or graph block starts about every 45 s in the 2021 videos** (15 blocks in Beluga, 12 in Squid Game) **and every 100 s in the 2019 one** (6 blocks). Inside those blocks there are about **2 different screenshots a minute** in the 2021 videos (Beluga and Squid Game) and about 1 a minute in the 2019 one.
- **A real graph about once every 4 minutes** (8 in 31 minutes). They always land on a turn: the jump in the hook (Beluga 0:27), "I've been there" (MrBeast 2:09), "had all but died down" (Squid Game 7:40).
- **The creator's clips come in 4-6 blocks per video**, each 13-28 s long and each announced: "Let's take a look", "Here's an example from one of his own videos", "Watch these first 30 seconds closely". Each video opens with one.
- **Step cards follow one template**: a white page, the creator's cut-out or avatar on one side, "STEP N" in black capitals and the step's name in teal underneath.
- **The last step gets the most screen time and the most screenshots.** It's the title-and-thumbnail breakdown in both 2021 videos, almost 2 minutes of labels built on one or two screenshots.
- **Cuts happen inside the creator's clips.** In Squid Game, 85 cuts were detected, nearly all inside the clip stretches (1:04-1:26, 2:00-2:58, 3:52-4:44). The 3.5-minute whiteboard stretch from 4:44 to 8:06 had one. The whiteboard is one long canvas that pans, zooms and wipes.

### How proof is shown
1. **Number first, highlight second.** A screenshot appears small and plain, then the camera pushes in and a hand-drawn ellipse is drawn round the number: "25M views", "$456,000", the subscriber count.
2. **Before → after on one picture.** A red arrow goes on the "before" and a green ellipse on the "after" (the Beluga graph). In MrBeast GENIUS the "before" is the creator's own old footage of 8,726 subscribers.
3. **A rival as the yardstick.** James Charles' tile with "4:40 MINS TO FIRST GAME" against "16 SECONDS"; Logan and Jake Paul in the bar chart.
4. **Let the subject prove it in his own voice**: Beluga's "fastest channel to hit 4 million", MrBeast's "I just reinvest everything".
5. **Outside data at the turn**: Google Trends "UPLOAD DATE", so the setback ("the trend had died") is shown, not only said.

### How it is dressed (what the existing notes missed or got slightly wrong)
- **Colours.** The "green" labels are a soft **teal/mint**, used for what works and for step names. The "red" is a soft **coral/pink**, used for the cost or the problem. Light **blue** is used for neutral ideas, mostly in the 2019 video. Black capitals are for headings. Highlights are **green ellipses** on numbers, **chunky red arrows** on the problem spot, **yellow ellipses and arrows** on thumbnail details, red underlines and red ticks.
- **Lettering.** Hand-lettered all-capital labels, 2-6 words, written on letter by letter (many frames catch a half-written word such as "TREND-JACKIN" or "GRO N"). Labels connect to their screenshot with thin, curved, hand-drawn black arrows.
- **Icons.** Two kinds: grey line drawings (crowds of people, the audience every time) and **flat full-colour icons** (gear, megaphone, headphones, brain, clock with red arrows, target, TV, potion). The notes said "black line icons"; in fact about half are full colour.
- **Screenshots** sit flat on white with a soft shadow, never full screen. The one exception is the watch page tilted in 3D at the start of Squid Game.
- **Cut-outs** are photos with the background removed and **no outline** inside the video (the coloured outline is only on his covers). Side characters go in **circles** (the cat avatar, the players' faces).
- **Labels over clips** are rare but used: "EDITING PACE / 30 SECONDS, 30 CUTS", the joke "(with no tripod lol)", a white drawn arrow over Beluga.
- **His style changed.** The 2019 video still uses clip-art cartoon people, a drawing hand and an ink splat. The two 2021 videos drop them for cleaner screenshots plus colour icons, and use more screenshots (31-36 % of frames against 14 %).

1. **Open on the subject's own footage and voice, then a number on screen within 20 s.** Beluga's face-reveal line, then his channel page at 0:21 ("4,700,000 subscribers").
2. **One growth graph in the hook, red arrow on "before", green ellipse on "after".** "BELUGA'S PLAN…" at 0:27.

## 3. Honest limits
- **One frame every 5 seconds.** Anything shorter than about 5 s can fall between frames. Shares are counts of sampled frames, so they are about ±2-3 points for each material, and short bursts are undercounted (the 5-s stock-footage burst at 2:11 in Beluga shows up in only one frame).
- **Exact cut timing between frames is unknown.** The cut times we kept come from a detector that misses whiteboard changes (zooms, pans and wipes don't count as cuts), so "shot lengths" for the whiteboard stretches are really "canvas lengths".
- **No motion.** We can't see how fast labels are written on, how the camera moves across the page, or which wipes are used. We only infer them from half-written words and zoomed-in frames.
- **No sound effects.** The kept sound data separates voice from everything else, and "everything else" includes the creators' clips, so we can't tell whooshes, pops or pen sounds apart from music.
- **Who is on screen.** The cut-out faces are matched to people from the transcript and the video titles on screen (for example "Beluga Face Reveal"). The YouTube Studio graph in the MrBeast video is read as Paddy's own channel because of what he says at that moment; it isn't labelled.
- **"Social Blade-style"** means the graph is titled and drawn like Social Blade's monthly-gains chart. The source isn't named on screen.
- **Three videos only**, two from 2021 and one from 2019. The other 7 hits weren't broken down frame by frame here.
