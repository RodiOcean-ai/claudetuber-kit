# MrBeast: the secret sauce (31 Oct 2026)

## The recipe in one paragraph
**Put something everyone wants in the frame, big, in the first second. Say the stake in the first breath. Then make the stake climb: each step bigger than the last. Hang everything on one "will it land?" try, and stop on the payoff, often with a joke sting.** A new picture every second, money and numbers glowing on screen, red arrows on the one detail that matters. He does it with real cash and real people. We do it with a 3D object and a number that climbs.

## Format, not luck
- #1 Short = **2.2 %** of all Shorts views; top 10 = **15 %**; median Short = **348M** across 205. (ColinAmazing: one clip = 39 %.) The format hits again and again.
- Remakes: 6 titles remade, every remake 209M or more, the remake won 3 of 6 ("Subscribe for an iPhone" 299M → 1.2B).
- **Decay:** median of the newest third is 146M, against 552M for the oldest third. It still works (newest best: 1.2B), but less often.

## What we copy (the craft)
1. **The climbing number tag:** a glowing price, view count or size tag on the hero object, climbing in 4-6 steps ($100K → $1M → ... → $100M). This is our "game board", the same idea as ColinAmazing's counter.
2. **The hero object fills frame one and moves by 0.4 s.** Not a wide shot, no presenter.
3. **A yes/no "Can you…?" / "Would you…?" line** fits our "open on a you-situation" rule. His best hook (8.8) is "Can you slice a bullet with a katana?".
4. **Show the payoff first for half a second** (the slow-motion katana shot at 0.8 s), then go back and build to it.
5. **A red arrow or circle** on the one small detail the viewer must watch.
6. **Faster pictures:** a new angle every 1.5-2 s inside our 4-second scenes (whips, push-ins, cutaways), not one slow drift.
7. **End on a sting, one dry line after the payoff,** then stop.
8. **Title formats:** "X vs Y" and "How many X to Y?" style questions, kept inside our question + one emoji pattern.

## What we don't copy
- His face, name, voice, catchphrases, real footage, thumbnails or logo. (Series rules: the creator stays anonymous until the last line; silhouettes and faceless toys only.)
- **Real money giveaways, "subscribe to win", sponsor reads, real people reacting.** That's his identity and his budget.
- **Loud music (+5 LU), no loop, 39-second length, hard cuts only.** Zack's rules win on format: +16 LU, loop to frame one, 27-35 s, whips.
- "Do X, Win $Y" titles: his most-used format, but below his median.

## How this changes our next Short
Open on **one hero object filling the frame with a glowing number tag**. **Climb the tag 4-6 times**, each step bigger, each picture 2 s or less. Hang the middle on **one "will it work?" moment**. Pay off at the highest number, add **one dry sting line**, and loop back to the object. Title: a "how many / X vs Y" style question with one emoji.

## Honest note
- His Shorts are reality clips. Our hook critic didn't mark him down for showing his face, but we can't use faces at all. Hook strength must come from the object and the motion, which is exactly what separated his hits from his misses.
- The 3 weak Shorts are a small contrast set. Patterns 4 and 6 need more misses before they count as certain.
- His views are falling in the newest third. He is a source of **structure**, not proof the format still grows.
