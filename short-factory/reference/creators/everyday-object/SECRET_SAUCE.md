# Nick DiGiovanni: the secret sauce (5 Nov 2026)

## The recipe in one paragraph
**Take a food every person on Earth knows (toast, pasta, nuggets, ice) and break one rule about it: make it gigantic, make it tiny, make it "gourmet", make it from a living chick, or let tiny chefs cook it inside the toaster. Bring a famous partner into the idea itself, not just into the title (Zach King, MrBeast, Lynja, Chef Rush). Say it as a mission in the first second ("Let's make…") or act it out with no words, climb step by step, and end on a joke that flips the scale ("Nick, why are yours so small?" "It's average.").** And never make the same idea twice: every weaker Short we checked repeats one of his own hits, and it lost 7-18 times the views.

## Format, not luck
- #1 Short = **3 %** of all 21 billion Shorts views; top 10 = **13 %**. The flattest channel we've studied (GothamChess 10.8 %, MrBeast 15 %, Paddy 78 % of long views in 10 videos).
- **A floor, not a lottery:** 489 Shorts, median **27M**, weakest **8.6M**.
- **Rising, not fading:** newest third median 34M, oldest third 20M.
- **But the template alone loses:** the same "Let's make the world's largest X (ft. Lynja)" script made 290M for sushi and 10M for a donut a year later; the identical cake-pop video re-posted as a "throwback" made 10M against 182M. The format is the floor; a new idea plus a famous partner makes the hit.

## What we copy (the craft)
1. **One everyday object, one broken rule, on frame one.** Our hero is an object everybody knows (a phone, a toaster, a play button) doing something impossible at 0 s. That's his #1 lesson and the hook critic's top-scoring pattern.
2. **A line said in the moment, not "Let's make…".** "Hey Chef, can you turn my ice gourmet?" (8.8) beats "Let's make the world's largest…" (5.7-6.4). For us: a "you" line with the object already moving.
3. **Never repeat our own idea.** Each episode gets a new hero object and a new rule-break. Our series pattern (title, look, reveal) stays the same; the idea inside never does.
4. **The joke sting after the payoff that flips the scale.** One short line after the reveal that turns the meaning around: our "bookend with a twist" rule, with a laugh.
5. **Borrowed fame inside the idea.** We can't show partners, but the creator we break down *is* our famous partner: the reveal in the last line is our "ft. MrBeast".
6. **Two words for the cover idea:** "GOURMET ICE", "SMALLEST PASTA". Our 0-3 glowing words should name the object and the rule-break.

## What we don't copy
- Real faces, real footage, his kitchen look, his name, his catchphrases. Faceless toy figures only; creators anonymous until the last line.
- **No music under the voice:** Zack's bed stays (voice +14-18 LU above it).
- **Cuts every 1.2 s:** our one-world flights stay.
- Luxury and money hooks ("$1 vs $100,000") in Shorts: below his own median (22.5M vs 27M). Good for long videos only.
- Giveaways, "1 view = 1 meal" asks, sponsor reads, own-brand product shots.
- Animal or "raising a chick to eat it" shock jokes: a dark sting can read as cruel without his face and tone.
