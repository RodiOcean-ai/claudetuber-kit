# MagnatesMedia thumbnail study (30 Oct 2026)
Channel: 1.86M subs, 226 videos. Pulled the 24 most-viewed (2.2M-12M views) and 12 most recent thumbnails (sheet_top.jpg, sheet_recent.jpg).

## The recipe
1. ONE hero, centred, big: a person's face (close, intense, often glowing teal/green eyes) or the company's logo as a 3D object (on fire, cracked).
2. The subject's logo/icon always in frame, so you know the topic in 0.1 s.
3. A "danger + money" prop layer: piles of green cash, blood drips, fire, skulls, handcuffs.
4. Hyper-saturated, over-sharpened painted look (recent ones are clearly AI-illustrated): red/yellow/green, deep shadows, strong rim light.
5. Text: 0-3 words. Recent thumbnails mostly have NO text; older ones use "THE TRUTH" / "DARK TRUTH" / one label in a coloured box (red, green, yellow) in a heavy sans.
6. Symmetry: subject in the middle, props balanced left/right, a loud background (flag, rays, fire).
7. A small "M" logo bottom-left on some older ones; dropped on new ones.
