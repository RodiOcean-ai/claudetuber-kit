# Veritasium: on-screen techniques with counts (15 Nov 2026)

Counted on his 10 most-viewed Shorts (24M-64M), 3 weak Shorts from the same channel (0.39M-0.69M, all 2026) and his 10 most-viewed long videos (40M-112M; openings = first 75 s). Whole-channel rows use all 97 Shorts and 448 long-video titles. Details and caveats in STUDY.md.

## Shorts: hits vs weak
| # | Technique | Hit Shorts (10) | Weak Shorts (3) | Mind trick | Can our lab Shorts use it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Frame one = a real object doing or about to do something (not a face, map or cartoon) | **7** | **0** | seeing is believing; the eye gets a question before the ear | Yes: frame one = the strangest real-looking object, already moving |
| 2 | The surprise happens on camera, against the obvious guess | **6** (5 real, 1 animated) | **0** | prediction error | Yes: show the wrong guess fail, don't say it |
| 3 | A wait-and-see moment (countdown, "let me see if...", "which one will fall faster?") | **8** | **0** | suspense felt in the body (mirror effect) | Yes: one held beat before the reveal |
| 4 | Voice stops 4 s+ while the thing happens | **4** (17, 6.5, 4.9, 4.7 s) | **0** (longest 1.3 s) | silence = something is about to happen | Yes, scaled: a 1-2 s silent beat in 30 s |
| 5 | Something at stake (his body, a priceless object, a telescope) | **5** | **0** | loss aversion, empathy | Yes: the hero risks something visible |
| 6 | Real people react or guess on camera ("That is trippy", strangers guessing) | **5** | **0** | emotional contagion, social proof | Yes: our side characters react (faceless toy figures) |
| 7 | 67 s or shorter | **9** | **0** (105-159 s) | completion urge | Already ours (30-35 s) |
| 8 | Ends on the peak (the surprise, the "no way", a laugh) | **7** | **0** (an ad, a summary, an anticlimax) | peak-end rule | Yes: last line = the reveal |
| 9 | Pays the title's promise inside the Short | **10** | **1** | a curiosity gap must close | Yes: never title what the Short doesn't show |
| 10 | No advert inside | **10** | **2** | reactance (feeling sold to) | Yes: no plugs in our Shorts |
| 11 | Spoken TEASE ("the answer begins with...", "there is a limit") instead of a visible wait | 0 | 2 | a promise in words is weaker than a pending event | Avoid: show the pending thing instead |
| 12 | Voice covers 90 %+ of the video | 2 | **3** | wall-to-wall talk = a lesson | Avoid |
| 13 | Arrows or A/B labels on the object | 4 | 0 | points the eye at what will surprise | Yes: our red arrow / ring |
| 14 | Open loop at the end ("Now, why did that happen?") | 1 (ladders: 49,000 comments, the most) | 0 | the unfinished question (Zeigarnik) drives comments and rewatches | Maybe once, if the "what" is fully shown |
| 15 | Outrage + tribe + call to act (#SaveChandra) | 1 (24M, best likes per view 5.0 %) | 0 | outrage, group identity | Not our format |
