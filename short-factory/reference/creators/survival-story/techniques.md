# Dr Plants: techniques (all 12 long videos, 17M-123M views)

The channel has **no Shorts**. Counted from his own uploaded captions (11 of 12; Ocean has automatic captions), Whisper transcripts, 1-frame-a-second sheets of the first 30 s, frame timelines, the voice/music split, the sound classifier, chapters and all 12 covers. "Strong" = the 9 videos that earned 2.1M+ views a month of age; "weak" = the 3 under 1.1M a month (Jungle 2022, Planet 2024, Frogs 2024). 6 November 2026.
