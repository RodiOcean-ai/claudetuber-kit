# Mark Rober study (11 Nov 2026)

Channel: @MarkRober, 86.2M subscribers. 132 long videos since Oct 2011 (about 9 a year), 6.39B views. 136 Shorts, 13.6B views.
Studied: top 10 long videos and top 10 Shorts by views, plus 4 recent flops the owner asked for (2 long, 2 Shorts). All 24 measured by machine (cuts, sound, Whisper words) and reviewed by Claude (openings, transcripts, thumbnails). List numbers are from YouTube on 11 Nov 2026 (one source).
Data: studies/markrober ("markrober - all data.xlsx", analysis/<id>/), copies of the workbook and sheets in this folder, story_blocks.png.

## Big picture
- **The streak:** his newest 85 long videos all have 10M+ views. It starts in June 2017. Usual video in the streak: 17 minutes, 52M views. (Corrected 11 Nov: the list gave no view count for 3 recent videos; their pages show 40.0M, 52.1M and 16.8M, so they are in the run. First count said 82.)
- **Before the streak:** 47 videos, usual length 3.8 minutes, mostly how-to demos ("Make a Gorilla cam- HOW TO", "LED Edge Lit Table- HOW TO"); 24 of 47 are under 10M.
- **He is slowing down, not speeding up:** newest 20 long videos median 34M (peak era 68M); his last 14 Shorts (Jun-Oct 2026) have 0.9M-40M each, most under 10M. His biggest Short (1.48B) is from Feb 2024.

Machine numbers (median of the 10 hits; flop 1; flop 2):
- Length 16.6 min; 21.2; 27.5.
- A cut every 2.0 s (22.5 cuts a minute, range 18-50); 1.6 s (28 a minute); 1.4 s (35 a minute). **The flops are cut faster, not slower.**
- Speaking speed 2.8 words a second; 2.9; 2.9. Voice over 73 % of the time; 77; 74.
- Music under 75 % of the time (70-84); 83; 75. About 5 sound effects a minute in all.
So pace, music and voice do not separate his hits from his flops. What differs is what the video is about and how it opens.

## The "I" title pattern (whole channel, list only)
Newest 25 long videos: 9 titles begin with "I" or "My" (median 27M); the other 16 name the thing or the opponent (median 45M). In the whole streak: 11 "I/My" titles, median 27M, against 74 others at 57M. Exception: his lowest recent long video is not an "I" title: "Can You Safely Drink Your Own Pee?" (1 Aug 2025, 16.8M; missed when the flops were picked because the list gave it no view count; not downloaded or measured). All 11 are among his newest 36 videos, so part of the gap is age and era.
Examples: "I Spent $5,000,000 So You Can Go To Space For FREE" 19M, "I Tried Building My Own Space Satellite" 21M, "I Engineered The Perfect First Date" 27M against "Octopus vs Underwater Maze" 118M, "Testing The World's Smartest Crow" 65M, "Lasers vs Lightning" 65M.

## Shorts: what the 10 hits share
- Median 54 s, a cut every 1.8 s, 3.3 words a second, music under 70 % of the time, voice on frame one in 9 of 10.
- **Frame one is one object in his hands or the stage for a stunt** (10 of 10): a slinky, a pound of jello, an egg, a jelly-bean jar, a turtle, a subscriber counter.
- **"This is X, and this is Y"** opens 3 of 10 (slinky -> infinite staircase; a pound of jello -> 30,000 pounds; an egg -> a bouncy egg).
- **A live number or a count that climbs** in 5 of 10: the subscriber counter (1.48B and 621M views), "the first of three" levels, the jelly-bean guesses, the percentages of drivers.
- **A childhood reason** in 2 ("as a kid I would always try...", "ever since I was a kid").
- **One or two things go wrong before it works** in 3 (slinky: treadmill, escalator, alignment; jello: 3,000 batches; MrBeast freezes).
- **The ending is a physical payoff or a sting**: confetti cannons, the foam explosion, the belly flop, "29,000 times more than I ever got as a kid", "Somebody subscribe!" (ends one subscriber short, twice).
- At least 2 retell an old long video in under a minute (the jello pool, turtles or snakes).

The two Short flops:
- "4 Levels of Satisfying Crunches" (1.2M): 39 words, music 16 % of the time, and the second sentence is an advert ("they're now in stores everywhere").
- "Stop Your Car From Getting Stolen!" (1.9M): opens on a statistic ("Over half a million cars are stolen every year"), then a how-to in steps. Well made (a cut every 1.4 s, 3.9 words a second), but it is a lesson with no stunt and no payoff moment: the format of his pre-2017 videos.

## Honest notes
- Top 10 by views favours older videos (2015-2024); both long flops are from 2025-26 and have had less time. The gap is real but part of it is age.
- Two flops of each kind is a small sample. The "I" title count is the only pattern tested on the whole channel, and it is tangled with era.
- Words are Whisper's; "setback" and "opponent" are Claude's reading of the transcripts and openings, not machine counts.
- "NASA engineer" and the subscriber and view numbers need a second source before a script.
- Views are not craft: topic, age and promotion are tangled. Say what the hits share and what the flops lack, never what caused the views.
