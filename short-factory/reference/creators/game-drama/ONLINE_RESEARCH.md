# GothamChess (Levy Rozman) - what others have already said about his methods and rise

Researched 3 Nov 2026. Read-only web research: nothing paid for, no sign-ins, nothing posted.

**How to read this:**
- Quotes are short (under 15 words) and copied exactly from the source as fetched.
- "Fan blog" means a page anyone can post on Chess.com or Medium. These are weak evidence, so they are marked as such.
- The NYT Magazine and the Guardian profiles block our reader. For those two I used summaries in other places (Wikipedia's citations, a Newser summary, Magzter's paywall teaser). They are marked "via summary".
- Subscriber counts come from trackers and change every day. Always check the live channel page before you lock a number in the script.

## 3. Facts that could open the Short (with sources)

**A. He is the biggest chess channel on YouTube, by a wide margin.**
- Wikipedia infobox (updated 13 Oct 2026): 8.2M subscribers, 6.3B views. Trackers show 8.1-8.3M (Social Blade achievements page; OutlierKit). You told me about 8.4M. Check the live page on the day you post.
- He became the biggest chess creator in Sept 2021, passing Agadmator: Wikipedia + Chess.com player page.
- ChessBase India (26 Jul 2026): it became the 3rd chess channel to reach 3M subscribers, after GothamChess and Hikaru - https://chessbase.in/news/chessbase-india-hits-3-million-subscribers

**B. The champions' own channels are far smaller.**
- Hikaru Nakamura (GMHikaru): about 3.2M (Wikipedia, 2026). June 2026 FIDE world No. 2-3 (rating 2792): ChessBase, June 2026 ratings.
- Magnus Carlsen: about 1.61M (Social Counts, 18 Jul 2026; vidIQ). He was world No. 1 at 2823 on the July 2026 list (Chess.com, ChessBase), after 15 straight years at No. 1.
- Agadmator (the old No. 1): about 1.4M (Wikipedia, Social Blade).
- **That means:** Levy has about 2.5 times Hikaru's subscribers and about 5 times Magnus's.

**C. He is not even a grandmaster, and is about 500 rating points below the world No. 1.**
- He is an International Master (IM, the title one step below grandmaster), earned in 2018. Peak FIDE rating 2421 (Aug 2018): Wikipedia; Chess.com player page (2420).
- Current FIDE rating 2318 (Feb 2026): Wikipedia. Magnus is at 2823 (Jul 2026), about 500 points higher.
- World rank: Newser (Dec 2023) says he ranked 6,689th in the world. I could not confirm his current FIDE world rank; the FIDE page did not load properly. Do not state an exact rank unless you check ratings.fide.com (player ID 2039877).

**D. From about 380,000 to 8 million+ in under six years.**
- Jan 2021: 379,000 YouTube subscribers (In The Know / Yahoo, 27 Jan 2021; Wikipedia).
- Mid-2021: 1M (Wikipedia; Creator's Digest; his own words to Lex Fridman: "I got to a million in a year").
- Twitch in 2020: about 17,800 followers at the start of the year, more than 100,000 by end of October (In The Know / Yahoo; Wikipedia).
- Views: first chess channel past 1 billion (Mar 2023, with 3.1M subs: Chessdom, 22 Nov 2024 - https://www.chessdom.com/gotham-chess-crossed-3-billion-views-on-youtube/; Chess.com, 5 Apr 2023). 3B by Nov 2024 (Chessdom). 6.3B by Oct 2026 (Wikipedia). The first billion took about five years; the next two took about 18 months (Chessdom).
- Subscribers before 2020: I found no reliable number.

**E. He wrote a New York Times bestseller.**
- How to Win at Chess (24 Oct 2023) reached No. 4 on the NYT "Advice, How-To & Misc." list and No. 1 on three Amazon lists: Chess.com, Tarjei J. Svensen, Nov 2023 - https://www.chess.com/news/view/gothamchess-releases-book-immediately-tops-amazon-lists; Tubefilter, 1 Dec 2023.
- More than 250,000 copies printed (printed, not necessarily sold), sold in 20 languages: Chess.com, 3 Mar 2026 - https://www.chess.com/news/view/levy-rozman-chess-for-babies
- A sequel ("Next Level") came out 6 Oct 2026 (Wikipedia; ChessBase India).

**F. He beat "the world": 225,136 people voting on moves.**
- Called the largest online chess match ever, beating the record from Magnus vs The World: Attacking Chess (30 Nov 2025). Chess.com news (8 Nov 2025) on the rook sacrifice. Wikipedia: over 225,000 players.

**G. Other recognition.** Forbes 30 Under 30 (2024, Games): Forbes + Chess.com news. Best Chess Streamer, Streamer Awards 2022 (Wikipedia; Chess.com, 5 Apr 2023).

## 4. Criticism (to stay balanced)

- **Clickbait titles and thumbnails.** This is the most common complaint. Someone even built a Chrome extension, "HonestLevy", that swaps his titles for plain ones (Chrome store listing). Reddit r/chess threads (May 2024) say the hype puts some viewers off; others reply that he narrates games better than anyone. These are fan opinions, not a survey.
- **GM Hans Niemann** hit back at a GothamChess video title, saying some "seek solace in YouTube clickbait" (as quoted in search coverage; I did not see the original post).
- **GM Ben Finegold** called him "the fraud he is" and challenged him to a match while trash-talking on a stream: Dexerto, Declan Mclaughlin, 24 Jul 2024 - https://dexerto.com/youtube/chess-gm-ben-finegold-challenges-fraud-youtuber-gothamchess-to-match-2835358 (Finegold is a known joker, so treat it as half banter).
- **Drama over accuracy.** One anonymous Reddit comment says he sometimes overstates moments for effect. Weak evidence.
- **The 2021 cheating backlash.** He reported an Indonesian player (Dewa_Kipas) for cheating. The account was closed, Indonesian users harassed him, and he went private for a while: Wikipedia, citing Wired (2021).
- **Big ego.** His NYT line "second-biggest entity in chess behind Chess.com" (via Newser), and on Lex Fridman: "I think I'm easily grandmaster level." Critics could use these to call him arrogant.
- **In his own defence:** "I get really sad when they make fun of me." (Chess.com, 5 Apr 2023)

## Strongest 5 facts for a hook

1. **The biggest chess channel on YouTube isn't run by a grandmaster.** Levy is an International Master, one step below grandmaster, with about 8.2M+ subscribers. The world No. 1, Magnus Carlsen, has about 1.6M. *Confidence: high* (Wikipedia Oct 2026; Social Counts / vidIQ Jul 2026; Chess.com player page). Check the live counts on posting day.
2. **He has about 5 times Magnus's subscribers and about 2.5 times Hikaru's**, even though Magnus is rated about 500 points higher (2823 vs 2318). *Confidence: high for subscribers, medium-high for ratings* (ratings move every month; Levy's 2318 is from Feb 2026).
3. **From about 380,000 subscribers in January 2021 to 8 million+ in 2026**, and he says he went from nothing to 1 million in about a year. *Confidence: high* (In The Know / Yahoo, Jan 2021; Wikipedia; Lex Fridman #327).
4. **His growth method in his own words: "I made two videos a day, every day, for a year."** *Confidence: high* (Tubefilter, 8 Jun 2023). It is his own claim, not counted by anyone else.
5. **First chess channel to pass 1 billion views (2023), and over 6 billion now**, plus a New York Times bestselling chess book for beginners (No. 4, Advice list, Nov 2023). *Confidence: high* (Chessdom; Chess.com; Tubefilter; Wikipedia).

Backup hook: "He beat 225,136 people at once": the largest online chess match ever, Nov 2025. *Confidence: high* (Attacking Chess; Chess.com; Wikipedia).
