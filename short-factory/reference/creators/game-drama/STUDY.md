# GothamChess study (@GothamChess, Levy Rozman, 8.41M subscribers)

A study of his 10 most-viewed Shorts (41M-59M), his 10 most-viewed long videos (6.4M-25M), 3 weaker Shorts in the same formats (0.1M-1.0M), and the titles and views of all 1,216 Shorts and 2,257 long videos. Made on 2 November 2026.

## The big picture
- An international master who turned chess into **drama with a scoreboard**. Every top Short is a small story about a person (him, Magnus, Hikaru, a bot, a 100-rated player) where something goes wrong and the board shows it.
- The channel's median Short gets 1.5M views; the top 10% get 8.9M+. The top 10 are 27-39 times the median, so the hits are a repeatable formula on top of a big base, not just luck.
- Shorts are **talk + board + face**, not animation. He is the narrator and the reaction shot. The chess is shown, never only told.

## Hooks (first 3 seconds): hook critic average **6.8/10** for the hits, **4.3** for the 3 weaker ones
1. **Voice on frame one with a feeling or rule-break word**: 8 of 10 hits ("I'm about to ruin the lives of millions of people", "There's a new rule in chess, apparently kings can touch?", "I hate this woman", "Magnus Carlsen is partying drunk", "I can't do this anymore"). Only 1 of 3 weaker ones.
2. **The subject is clear by about 1.6 s** (the board, the chess.com logo, or chess said aloud): 7 of 10 hits, 0 of 3 weaker. The best proof: "I'm done with this." (44M) and "I'm done. Bye." (1.0M) open with **the same line**. The hit shows the chess.com logo and "a bot of me" at 1.6 s. The miss stays on a still face and doesn't name its subject until 3 s. The critic scored them 7.7 and 5.2.
3. **One specific question planted by 3 s**: 8 of 10 hits, 0 of 3 weaker. "Made a bot of me" (how bad is it?), "kings can touch?" (is that legal?). The weaker "feels illegal to know?" is a stock trend line, not a specific question.
4. **A famous name or face inside 3 s** (Magnus, Hikaru, Botez, chess.com): 6 of 10. It carries two weak hooks (4.8 each) to 41M+. **For us that's a warning, not a tool**: we can't show real people. A famous object or brand-free stand-in has to do the stopping.
5. **A live number on screen** in 3 of 10. "3000 Elo" (56M, 13 words spoken in total!) is just an "Elo:" counter over a man at a board. It jumps 0 → 2300, then falls 1200 → 800 → 500 → 200 → 100 as he plays worse and worse, then snaps to "Elo: 3000" with a skull for the payoff. The number tells the whole story.

## Script formula (story blocks, chart in story_blocks.png)
| Block | Hits (of 10) | Weaker (of 3) |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 10 | 3 |
| **SETBACK** (it goes wrong: a blunder, the bot betrays him, the site is blocked) | **7** | **0** |
| "BUT" twist | 6 | 0 |
| TENSION (clock running, "will he?") | 6 | 0 |
| LADDER (each step stranger) | 6 | 2 |
| PAYOFF | 9 | 3 |
| TEASE | 4 | 1 |
| COMEBACK | 2 | 0 |

- **The hits are stories, the weaker ones are lessons.** All 3 weaker Shorts go hook → steps → payoff with nothing going wrong. 7 of 10 hits have a setback and 6 have a "But" turn. "WIN AT CHESS In 8 Moves!" (44M) works as a lesson only because it opens as a scene ("Chess is too hard." "Well, here's a way…") and sets a trap the opponent falls into. The 0.8M "5 moves" version is the same lesson without the scene.
- The pattern: **person + a rule or record + it goes wrong + it lands anyway**. "Drunk Magnus" is losing with 20 seconds left, gives the queen back, then wins with a pawn checkmate.
- The payoff comes in the last 3-5 s and the Short stops right there, in 7 of 10.

## Footage
- **Face to camera** (close, eyes in the lens, plain room) opens 6 of 10.
- **Stacked screen** in 5 of 10: the online board on top, his face or the real tournament room below. The board shows the move and the face shows the feeling.
- **Real event footage** in 3 of 10 (a tournament hall, a real clock, a real board).
- **Cut-ins for 1-2 s** in 4 of 10: a screenshot, a meme (Drake "no/yes"), a "this site can't be reached" page, a photo with a red arrow.
- Black-and-white "sad" opening that snaps to colour in 2 of 10 ("I'm sorry", "I'm done").
- Board highlights: yellow squares for the last move, a red arrow or circle for the key piece.

## Transitions
Hard cuts only. No whips or fly-throughs. The pace comes from the board moving and the cut-ins.

## Sound
- Music under 91-100 % of every hit, a light beat (83-152 BPM), only ~10 LU under the voice.
- 6 sound effects a minute (median): dings, bangs, snaps, ticking clocks. The skit Short "I Challenged Magnus" uses 23 a minute (snaps and slaps on every move). "Sigma Magnus" uses a tick-tock as the clock runs.
- His voice: fast, stressed words about 38 a minute, few fillers in the scripted ones.

## Captions
1-3 words at a time, white bold with a dark outline, centre or upper middle, in 9 of 10. Colour words now and then ("chess" in green, "CHESS IS TOO HARD" in red). Built to read on mute.

## Titles and covers
- **3 words is his median Shorts title.** Formats with the highest median views (all 1,216 Shorts, channel median 1.5M):
  - "WIN / CHECKMATE in N moves": 13 Shorts, **median 6.0M** (four times the channel)
  - Contains a number: 112, median 2.5M; opens with "The": 45, median 2.6M
  - Contains "cheat": 21, median 2.8M; has "!": 109, median 2.6M
  - Has "?": 124, median 1.45M; has an emoji: 102, median 1.4M. **Questions and emoji do slightly worse than average for him.**
- The top titles read like a feeling or a reaction, not a description: "I am sorry for this.", "I'm done with this.", "I hate this lady". The same style is only average across the channel (32 Shorts, median 1.9M). They work when the video delivers a story.
- Long videos: "Guess the Elo" (104 videos, median 1.2M), "learn" (1.4M) and "how to" (0.98M) beat his long median of 0.54M. Bot battles beat it too: "bot" 0.97M, "Can X…?" 0.79M. Titles name a challenge with a number: "30 Queens. Can Martin Win?", "30 Brilliant Moves IN A ROW".
- Long covers: his face (shocked or puzzled) next to a **cartoon bot character** or a board, 1-2 big words ("OPENINGS?", "CHESS?", "VS"). Shorts covers are just frames from the video.

## Long videos (10 of 10 reviewed from transcripts and openings)
- **Cold open names the challenge in the first sentence** in 8 of 10: "Ladies and gentlemen…" + who is fighting whom ("Mittens… rated 1… beats all the strongest grandmasters"; "30 queens against Martin").
- **Built as a ladder of opponents** in 5 of 10. Each chapter is a harder bot: 1300 → 1600 → 1900 → 2000 → 2200, or "Beth age 8 → 9 → 10 → 15 → 17 → 20 → final mode".
- **Payoff at the very end**, in the final game or the final bot.
- **Sponsor is light**: his own courses and tour in the description and a short mention. No mid-roll read in 8 of 10.
- **Ending**: 4 of 10 end by asking viewers to choose what comes next in the comments ("would you like to see Martin get his revenge? let me know in the comments", "which openings do you want me to cover next"). His trademark sign-off is "Get out of here!"
- Long videos are one take with very few cuts (one about every 5-10 min). The board is the picture.

## Honest note
- Cut counts undercount: a piece moving inside a screen recording isn't a "cut". The real picture-change rate was read from the timelines.
- Loop seam was measured on 2 Shorts. Both are clear jumps, and none of the 10 is built to loop.
- His Shorts lean on real famous players. We can't copy that. Our version must use faceless figures and a famous stand-in (the board, the clock, the rating number).
- Like and comment counts are from the download time. "NEW CHESS UPDATE", which ends "Do you agree?", has the most comments of the top 10 (14,000).
