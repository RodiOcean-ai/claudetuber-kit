Techniques (seen on screen):
1. A game board on screen in 6 of the 8 hits: the viewer always knows the rules and how many are left, so they wait for the last one.
2. Built for mute: every rule is written on screen.
3. Everyday objects a kid owns (bottle, pool, stairs); the 8M flop used an arcade machine and ran 33 s.
4. Short and dense: the six biggest run 15-26 s, a new item every 2-3 s.
5. Remake the winner: 10 Ways to Flip a Bottle (806M) remade as 10 Coolest Ways (802M); pool x2; emoji baseball x2 (601M, 437M); 13 "10 Ways to..." Shorts.
6. A task that needs a rewatch: "Count the bounces... How many?"
