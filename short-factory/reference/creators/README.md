# Channel studies

`/study-channel` writes what it learns about each channel here, one folder per study: the study notes (`STUDY.md`),
the "secret sauce" (what repeats in their hits), techniques and story-block charts. The downloaded videos themselves stay
in your studies folder and are deleted after measuring. Your own new studies go here too (pick a short key, e.g.
`somechannel`); when **sf-secret-sauce** turns one into a method skill and critic, add a row to the table below.

The studies that come with the kit are in folders named after the method each became. The skill and critic for a study
are its pair: sf-pipeline uses this table (and the tracker's Skills & experts sheet) to find the episode's method skill
and method critic.
