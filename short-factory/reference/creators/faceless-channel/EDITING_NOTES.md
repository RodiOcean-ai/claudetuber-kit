All 9 videos: 1920×1080, 60 fps. Times are m:ss. "Music drop" = background stem falls 8+ dB, then returns. Cut counts miss animated transitions (mind-maps, line-art and zooming screen recordings read as one long shot), so the real visual change rate is higher than the cut rate.

Rough age order: I05, I06 (oldest) → I01, I08, I02 → I03, I07, I09, I04 (newest). The style moves from "mascot in a suit + big captions + stock footage" to "gold line-art skits + round avatar + kinetic serif type".

## 2. House style: a spec to rebuild in Remotion

**Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps. Long videos run 11-22 min (median 17.5); the older ones run 5-6 min.

**Pacing (overall):** 10.7-22.0 cuts/min, median 14.0. Average shot 2.7-5.6 s (median 4.3); median shot 1.4-2.8 s (median 2.1). 10-28% of shots are under 1 s. 9-14% of shots are over 10 s, but they fill 36-60% of the runtime.

**Pacing by section:**
- Tutorial / screen recording: 10-90 s with no cut, kept alive by eased zooms, pans, a slow 3D tilt/drift and pop-in labels. Something new appears every 2-4 s.
- Example / montage bursts: 0.5-1.5 s per shot (20-29 cuts in 30 s).
- Line-art skits: 3-6 s per drawing, or long dissolves with no hard cut (endings).
- Chapter mind-map: one continuous animated move of 10-60 s.
- Cold open: first hard cut at 2-15 s (median ~3 s); the movement happens inside one layout.

**Text rules:**
- Captions: white bold sans, 1-4 words, centred at ~88% height, dark shadow. Big (~6% of frame height) in older videos, small (~3%) in newer ones, and sparser in newer ones.
- Punch words: centre, ALL CAPS heavy geometric sans, white or yellow with a soft yellow glow; one word per beat, often on black.
- Story/line-art subtitles: tiny italic serif, cream/amber, bottom centre.
- Newer kinetic type: white sans mixed with yellow/red italic serif or script; words stacked and scaled; hand-drawn red circles, arrows and underlines on UI.
- Comment cards (dark rounded box, avatar, @handle) fading in on black: his signature opener (I02, I03, I07, I09).

**Mascot (ginger, bearded, in a suit):**
- Full-body painted cut-out, entering from the right edge and taking up 40-100% of the frame height; points at or presents the content. Every 10-30 s in I01, I02, I05, I06 and I08.
- Round painted-head avatar badge in a corner of screen recordings (newer videos).
- Gold line-art version for skits; AI-image scenes in I08.
- Outfits change (grey, navy or black suit; shirt; flannel). Face and hair stay the same.

**Overlay/transition kit:**
- Orange/yellow light-leak flash, screen-blended (about one per 3-5 min).
- Zoom-blur push (radial).
- 3D-tilted screen recordings.
- Neon outline frames.
- Drawn glowing lines (mind-maps, roadmaps, scribble wipes).
- Ember/spark particles, glass shatter, dust/grain, RGB glitch, pixel mosaic.
- Hard cut to a single word on black.

**Colour:** base black (#000-#111). Primary gold/amber (~#FFC400 / #F5A623). Secondary cyan (~#19E3FF). Accent red (~#FF2A2A) for warnings and arrows. Green only in I02/I07. White text. Line-art skits go amber → red-orange at the low point → yellow at the resolution.

**Music plan:**
- A bed runs under 83-94% of the runtime (median 90%), mostly upbeat at 117-172 BPM (median ~152; the tempo detector is rough). I06 uses one calm ~80 BPM bed.
- 5-30 drops per long video. The typical move: the bed cuts out (to −40 to −60 dB or silence) 1-3 s before or on a punchline, meta joke ("turn off the music") or skit line, then slams back +10 to +20 dB (up to +42) on the next sentence.
- Sponsor (.store, usually 3:00-5:00): played as a skit with 2-3 drops on the punchlines. The bed returns on the line after "Huge thanks to…".
- The turn into the emotional ending: 7-8 s of near-silence on a "But…" line, then a new soft bed (piano, horns) under line-art.
- Openers: either quiet (−28 to −50 LUFS short-term over the first 3 s, voice at 2-3 s) or sound effects only, with no voice for 6-10 s (I03, I09). Older videos start talking at 0.0 s.

**Sound effects:** 2.2-9.5 per minute counted (median 4.3); the real number is higher because the detector catches only clear ones. Kit:
- whoosh/swipe on transitions;
- click or camera shutter on pop-ups;
- typing under screen recordings;
- boom/explosion/riser on reveals;
- heartbeat for tension;
- coin/cash register for money;
- gasp;
- comic stingers (French-horn blat, double-bass pluck, animal noises, ice-cream-truck jingle).

**Loudness:**
- Mix integrated −17.7 to −22.3 LUFS, median −18.0.
- Voice −17.8 to −22.5 (median −18.9).
- Bed + effects −24.6 to −31.3 (median −28.1).
- Voice over background: 5.6-12.2 LU, median 8.8. Target ~9 LU, 6 for hype, 11-12 for teaching.
- Loudness range 2.3-6.9 LU (median 4.7).
- True peak reaches +0.9 dBTP: he clips, so cap at −1 dBTP instead.

**Voice speed:** 2.4-3.7 words/s, median 3.2. The voice covers 68-89% of the runtime.
